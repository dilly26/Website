FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Use the contact form on the home page.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no account, no sign-up and no payment. Open it and start typing.

Where is my information stored?

Only in your own browser. Your CV is autosaved on your device and nothing you type is sent to a server. Use Save project to download a backup you can import later, because clearing your browser data also clears the autosave.

Which formats can I download?

PDF (through your browser's print dialog, so the text stays searchable), Word (.docx), LaTeX (.tex) for Overleaf, a Europass-style PDF, and your CV data as XML. The Europass-style layout is not an official Europass document.

Can I add my own sections?

Yes. Add custom sections for teaching, fieldwork, volunteering, presentations and more, and hide any section you don't need.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, it works on phones and tablets. A laptop or desktop is the most comfortable, because the form and the A4 preview sit side by side.
