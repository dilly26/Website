About Me

Making streets work better for everyone who walks, drives and rides.

I'm a Transportation Engineering M.Tech student at IIT Roorkee. I'm fascinated by how people read the road and make decisions on it, and I enjoy turning those insights into practical, people-first ideas.

Research interests

How people see the road, and how they decide

My work looks at how pedestrians and drivers read the street, how much they trust the information they get, and how that shapes the choices they make.
